## What it does

Toggles plan mode for multi-step planning.

Moves the chat into or out of a planning workflow. Use it to work through the approach and important decisions before implementation, or before turning the result into a persistent `/goal`.

## Reach for it when

- A change spans several files or has sequencing decisions
- You want to refine an objective before setting a long-running goal
