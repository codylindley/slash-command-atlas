## What it does

Opens the feedback dialog, with an option to include logs.

Starts the in-app feedback flow so you can describe a problem or share product feedback. Logs are optional and are included only through that dialog.

## Reach for it when

- A Codex action behaved unexpectedly
- You want to report a reproducible app or agent issue and attach diagnostics
