## What it does

Opens the model picker.

Auto can route each prompt with an Efficiency, Balance, or Intelligence preference. These prioritize cost, balanced cost/quality/latency, and quality respectively, while using the same model pool eligible for your plan and policies. The tier controls model routing, not a model’s reasoning-effort level.

> **Note:** The three Auto tiers began rolling out to VS Code on 2026-09-14 and may not yet appear in your installation. Intelligence can still choose a smaller model for a straightforward prompt.
